The process

What working with WeFlair actually looks like.

Five stages. Clear owners. Real outputs. From first audit through ongoing compounding, the engagement stays visible, structured, and tied to commercial outcomes.

Week 1
Access Audit Diagnosis

Discovery & Audit

We get access to your stack, audit where things are leaking, and diagnose the gaps before anyone starts executing. By end of week one you know what is broken, what is working, and what needs fixing first.

  • Connect ad accounts, CRM, analytics, landing pages, and creative environments.
  • Audit the leaks across paid, funnel, CRM handoff, and follow-up sequences.
  • Map where spend is wasted, where leads drop off, and where attribution is blind.
Output

A full diagnostic showing every leak, gap, and missed opportunity across your growth stack.

Weeks 3–4
Campaigns Creative Tracking

Build & Setup

Campaigns get built, creative gets produced, landing pages go live, and tracking is wired end-to-end. Nothing launches until every piece is connected and the measurement layer can prove what is working.

  • Build campaign structures across paid, outbound, and lifecycle channels.
  • Produce ad creative, email sequences, and landing page variants.
  • Wire conversion tracking, UTM structures, and CRM handoff so attribution is clean from day one.
Output

Launch-ready campaigns with creative, landing pages, and full tracking connected before a single euro is spent.

Weeks 4–12
Campaigns Optimisation Scaling

Execution & Optimisation

Campaigns launch, optimisation cycles run weekly, and scaling happens on live signal instead of stale reporting decks. The pod ships across every channel, adapts when priorities shift, and tightens performance in real time.

  • Launch campaigns across paid, outbound, content, lifecycle, and landing page conversion.
  • Optimise weekly using live data, call feedback, and real business constraints.
  • Scale what works and cut what does not, without waiting through layers of approval.
Output

Visible launches, faster feedback loops, and a system that keeps learning instead of stalling after kickoff.
