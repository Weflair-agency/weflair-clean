FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes B2B hardware marketing so complex?

You’re selling high-consideration products, often to technical buyers, through long sales cycles. The stakes are high, the buying committees are large, and the value needs to be crystal clear. That’s why your marketing needs to educate, build trust, and create demand - long before someone fills out a form.

How do you help hardware companies grow pipeline?

We combine content and performance marketing to make you top-of-mind in your niche and capture in-market demand. From SEO and CRO to video and direct outreach, we create a system that consistently drives qualified leads and real business opportunities.

Can you help us explain technical products in a clear way?

Absolutely. We specialize in turning complex ideas into compelling, easy-to-understand messaging and visuals. Whether through motion design, thought leadership, or product videos - we make your value impossible to ignore.

Do you work with industrial, manufacturing, and tech hardware brands?

Yes. We’ve helped companies in manufacturing, IoT, logistics, industrial automation, and high-tech hardware. If your product requires explanation, and your buyers do serious research before they purchase, we’re a good fit.

How do you reach technical buyers or engineers?

We build campaigns that combine trusted channels (like Google Search, LinkedIn, and YouTube) with high-value content and precise targeting. We speak their language, focus on use cases, and showcase proof.

What kind of results can I expect?

We’ve helped B2B hardware brands cut CPL by 80%, grow organic traffic by 130%+, and drive hundreds of high-quality leads across markets. See our work with FOCUS-ON, Logitech, and Movella for real-world examples.

Can you support global campaigns?

Yes. We’ve run campaigns in over 20 countries and adapt content, language, and targeting by region. We understand how to scale marketing while staying relevant locally.

Do you work with distributors or only direct-to-customer brands?

Both. Whether you sell directly or through partners, we help you build awareness, generate demand, and support your sales ecosystem - from end-user marketing to channel support.

Do you help with product-focused creative?

Yes. We create product demos, explainer videos, animations, and talking-head content that simplifies complex features and drives action. We can shoot on-site or in our in-house studio.

Can you help track ROI across long sales cycles?

Definitely. We set up tracking systems, dashboards, and attribution models that give you a clear view of what’s working—even when deals take months to close.

What does a collaboration cost?

We always set-up a Demand Team. The cost of the Demand Team depends completely on what type of experts you want on your team and how fast we want to improve your marketing. You can hire a Demand Team for €6240 per month onwards.

How quickly can we get started?

This depends on the availability of the Dapper team, and more specifically the specialist you need. Usually we can get a Demand Team up and running within a month. Get in touch to find out more.
