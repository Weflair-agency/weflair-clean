The Real Problem

Why most e-commerce brands plateau

Growth stalls when the tactics that worked at £1M revenue stop working at £5M. The channels are the same — but the leverage points shift entirely.

ROAS is lying to you

Last-click attribution inflates channel performance. You're spending more on ads that look profitable but can't survive contribution-margin analysis.

Cart abandonment is haemorrhaging revenue

70%+ of shoppers abandon before purchasing. Most teams send one recovery email and move on. The real lift is in the full checkout experience and post-abandon sequence.

CAC is rising with no ceiling in sight

Meta and Google CPMs have climbed 40–60% in three years. Brands with no LTV model keep increasing budgets to maintain volume — eventually the maths breaks.

One-time buyers who never come back

Brands live and die by repeat purchase rate. When 85% of customers never buy again, you're running on an acquisition treadmill with no way to reduce spend.

Creatives burning out faster than ever

Ad fatigue hits in days, not weeks. Teams with no structured creative testing cycle are outpaced by competitors who've built systematic approaches to ideation and iteration.

Reporting shows traffic, not profit

GA4 and ad dashboards give you the inputs, not the outcomes. Without contribution-margin reporting tied to channels, you're optimising for the wrong number.

Our Process

How we improve e-commerce growth

Four phases. No retainers that run on autopilot. Every engagement is built around finding the highest-value fixes and executing them fast.

01 / Audit

Find the bleed before we fix the brand

We audit your full acquisition and conversion stack: channel attribution, creative performance, funnel drop-offs, lifecycle gaps, and contribution margin by channel. No guessing — just data.

02 / Strategy

Fix the path, not just the campaign

We define your ICP by LTV cohort, identify the highest-value fixes, and build a 90-day roadmap with clear revenue targets. Paid, lifecycle, CRO, and creative all get clear owners and priorities.

03 / Execute

Launch, test, and iterate at pace

We move in two-week sprints. Campaigns live within 10 days. Creative tests run weekly. Lifecycle flows deployed and measured. We act like your internal growth team, not an external agency.

04 / Optimise

Report to revenue, not activity

Weekly reporting tied to contribution margin, not vanity metrics. Monthly strategic reviews that adjust the roadmap based on what's working. You always know what's running and what's next.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What types of e-commerce brands do you work with?
We work with DTC and marketplace-integrated brands generating between £1M and £30M in annual revenue. Our sweet spot is brands that have proven product-market fit and are looking to scale profitably — not just at volume. We've worked across apparel, home & living, health & wellness, beauty, food & drink, and sporting goods.
What platforms and tools do you work with?
Natively: Shopify, WooCommerce, Klaviyo, Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, GA4, Northbeam, Triple Whale, and Looker Studio. We don't impose a tech stack — we work in yours and fill gaps where needed.
How quickly will we see results?
Paid media optimisations typically show measurable lift within the first 30 days. CRO and lifecycle programmes compound over 60–90 days as test cycles run and flows mature. Most clients see a clear ROI-positive return within the first quarter. We'll set specific milestones before we start so you know exactly what to expect.
Do you manage creative as well as media buying?
Yes — and it's one of the things that separates us from most agencies. Creative and media buying are run from the same team with shared data. We handle concept, brief, and production (static and motion), and build structured testing frameworks so every creative informs the next.
What does a typical engagement look like?
Engagements start with a two-week audit and strategy phase, followed by a 90-day execution sprint. We work on retainer (typically £5,000–£15,000/month depending on scope) with a minimum three-month commitment. We're selective about who we take on — we'd rather work with fewer clients and go deep than run a high-volume account model.
How is WeFlair different from a traditional paid media agency?
Most paid media agencies optimise for the metrics they control: ROAS, CTR, and CPM. We optimise for contribution margin and LTV. That means we work across paid, lifecycle, and CRO simultaneously — because acquisition efficiency is useless if conversion and retention are broken. We report in the numbers your CFO cares about, not just ad platform dashboards.
What's the minimum budget to work with you?
For paid media management, we generally recommend a minimum ad spend of £10,000/month to generate statistically meaningful test cycles. For CRO and lifecycle-only engagements, there's no ad spend minimum. If you're below these thresholds, book a call anyway — we can often point you toward the right next step even if it's not a full engagement.
