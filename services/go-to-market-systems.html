GTM & Outbound Questions.

How fast can we see pipeline growth?

Domain setup and warming takes 14 days minimum. During this time we build the data tables and write copy. You will start seeing active multichannel outreach by week 3, and pipeline converting reliably by week 5-6.

Do you charge per lead or a retainer?

We charge a flat monthly retainer for operating the system. Building elite infrastructure requires heavy upfront engineering ops. We don't do "pay per meeting" with zero retainer, but we do operate with strict performance SLAs.

Will this hurt our main domain sender reputation?

No. We buy, configure, and warm up entirely separate domains (e.g., getyourbrand.com if your brand is yourbrand.com) and set up DMARC, DKIM, and SPF records perfectly. Your main domain is entirely insulated.

We already have SDRs. Do we fire them?

No! If you have closers or SDRs, our system acts as their superpower. We feed them highly-scored intent lists and manage the initial touchpoints. SDRs can shift to focusing entirely on warm conversations and complex account-based selling rather than copy-pasting emails.
