Frequently asked questions.

What ad spend do you recommend to start?
It depends on channel and ICP. For B2B LinkedIn, we typically recommend a minimum of €3K/month in ad spend. For ecommerce Meta, £2K is enough to validate. We'll model expected returns during the strategy call so you know the numbers before committing.
How quickly can we expect results?
Campaigns go live within 14 days of kickoff. Initial signal (CPL trends, creative winners) appears in weeks 2–4. Meaningful pipeline impact typically shows by week 6–8. We set clear milestones so there's never ambiguity about whether things are working.
Do you handle creative or do we need to provide it?
We handle everything in-house — ad copy, static designs, motion, video, and landing pages. Our creative team ships new variations every sprint as part of the engagement. You approve, we iterate.
How do you measure success?
Pipeline-qualified metrics: cost per qualified lead, pipeline ROAS, CAC, and closed/won revenue attributed to paid. We connect ad data to your CRM so every metric traces back to actual revenue — not just clicks or form fills.
Can you audit our existing paid setup first?
Yes — most engagements start with a paid audit. We'll identify what's working, what's burning budget, and where the biggest opportunities are. If there's a fit, we'll present a roadmap. If not, we'll say so.
What platforms do you manage?
Google Ads, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), LinkedIn, TikTok, Reddit, YouTube, Microsoft Ads, programmatic display, and connected TV. We recommend the channel mix based on your ICP and growth stage — not a one-size-fits-all playbook.
