Tracking & Self Reported Attribution

For years, marketers have focused on metrics like clicks, whitepaper downloads, and website visits. But while these numbers can be useful, they don’t tell the full story.

Define ICP

Every business has an Ideal Customer Profile (ICP). But chances are, yours is not well-defined enough - making it tough to create marketing that resonates and converts.

Collect Data

In digital marketing, research might not be the flashiest element, but it's essential for a successful strategy.

Define SMEs and Distributors

Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) are the people in your organization who deeply understand the market, your product, and the challenges of your prospects. Often, these are founders or long-time experts with invaluable insights.

Define Core Messaging

This is the step where you decide what you want to become famous for in your niche. To stand out, you need a few clear categories and topics - each with a strong, consistent point of view. Sticking to these in your messaging helps build recognition and authority. Talking about everything means standing for nothing.

Define Content Strategy

With so many content options - podcasts, short-form videos, blogs, and more - it’s easy to feel overwhelmed. But the key is to map out your capabilities and match them with your goals.

Define Demand Gen Channels

There are countless options - especially when repurposing content across platforms. Will the podcast live only on Spotify, or will you share snippets on LinkedIn? Should the full podcast video also go on YouTube?
