Auditing & analysis

AI search optimization starts with a full audit of how your brand shows up across ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Overviews, Perplexity, and classic search. Visibility & optimization

Conversion optimization

Testing & reporting

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about AI search, GEO, and SEO.

The language is changing fast. The mechanics underneath it are more stable than most people think.

What is the difference between AI search optimization and generative engine optimization?

Most teams use the terms interchangeably. We treat both as the work of helping your brand get understood, cited, and clicked across answer engines. The label matters less than the mechanics: structure, clarity, authority, and what happens after the visit lands.

How is this different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO focuses on rankings and organic traffic. This program still covers that layer, but it also accounts for how pages are summarized, cited, and selected inside AI interfaces, plus whether those visits actually convert once they arrive.

Why do brand mentions and citations matter so much for AI visibility?

Models infer trust from the wider web, not just your site. Strong mentions, backlinks, PR, and clean brand-topic association reinforce why your brand should be surfaced for a category, question, or recommendation prompt.

What technical elements make content more AI-ready?

Clear heading structure, crawlable HTML, schema, entity clarity, internal linking, concise summaries, comparison blocks, and pages that connect intent to proof all make it easier for both engines and models to parse and reuse your content.

How do you measure whether AI search work is actually paying off?

We look at citation visibility, referral sessions, on-page engagement, assisted conversion patterns, and the prompts or pages generating commercial opportunity. Visibility without pipeline movement is not enough.

Want to know where your brand is invisible, misrepresented, or easy to beat?

We will audit how you show up across search and AI surfaces, identify where competitors are outranking or out-citing you, and show the changes most likely to move visibility and conversion together.
