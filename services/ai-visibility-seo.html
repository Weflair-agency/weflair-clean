The opportunity

Search is becoming a recommendation layer. Your site is only one signal.

AI does not simply rank a page. It pieces together a story from your site, third-party mentions, structured content, reviews, communities, videos, and old SEO signals. If that story is unclear, outdated, or weaker than a competitor's, you lose before the visit ever starts.

Problem

AI answers skip weak brands.

If your positioning, proof, and entity signals are thin, models recommend competitors with clearer evidence.

Risk

SEO-only work is too narrow.

Classic SEO still matters, but it does not cover answer structure, citation logic, offsite presence, or the post-click path.

Upside

Good signals compound.

Clear content, structured data, authority mentions, and conversion-focused pages reinforce each other over time.

FAQ

Common questions about AI search optimization.

The labels keep changing. The underlying work is practical: make your brand easier to understand, cite, recommend, and trust.

Is this different from SEO?

Yes, but it does not replace SEO. We still need technical health, crawlability, content depth, and rankings. AI search adds answer structure, citation logic, entity clarity, third-party proof, and post-click conversion.

What does the free audit include?

We review how your brand appears across key AI and search surfaces, compare that against competitors, identify the pages and sources shaping the answers, and show the fixes most likely to improve visibility and conversion.

Do you write AI content?

We use AI to speed up research and analysis, but the deliverable is not generic generated content. The work is structured pages, stronger evidence, better answers, useful resources, schema, and authority signals that buyers can trust.

Why do third-party mentions matter?

AI systems infer trust from the wider web. Reviews, communities, publications, videos, partner profiles, and comparison pages can all influence whether your brand is recommended or ignored.

How do you measure success?

We look at AI answer presence, source citations, organic rankings, referral sessions from AI platforms, engagement quality, assisted conversions, and the pages or prompts that produce commercial opportunities.
