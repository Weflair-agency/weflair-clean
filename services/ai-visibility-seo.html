02 · How search changed

The new search reality.

Search became a recommendation engine. You’re one signal out of many. A modern buyer asks the model for an answer, not a list of blue links — that answer is stitched together from your site, third-party mentions, reviews, communities, comparison pages, and old SEO signals. Weak in two or three of those, and the answer recommends someone else.

01

AI skips brands with thin proof.

If positioning, reviews, and entity signals are weak or contradictory, the model defaults to whoever's documented better — usually a competitor with a stronger offsite trail.

ImpactPipeline lost before the buyer ever sees your site.
02

SEO alone can't fix this.

Classic SEO rebuilds rankings. AI visibility needs answer structure, citation logic, and earned third-party presence — none of which a sitemap or a keyword cluster will move.

Compounding signals own the long game.

Clear pages, structured data, earned mentions, and conversion-ready landings reinforce each other. Models notice the pattern. Buyers do too. The flywheel stops needing paid pressure to keep turning.

07 · Common questions

FAQ.

The labels keep changing — AEO, GEO, LLMO. The underlying job stays the same: make your brand easier to understand, cite, recommend, and trust.

How is this different from SEO?
It doesn't replace SEO. You still need technical health, crawlability, content depth, and rankings. AI visibility adds answer structure, citation logic, entity clarity, third-party proof, and post-click conversion — the parts a sitemap can't reach.
What does the free audit actually include?
A read on how your brand appears across the major AI and search surfaces, a side-by-side against the competitors winning the prompts you should be winning, the pages and sources currently shaping the answer, and a prioritised list of fixes most likely to move visibility and pipeline first.
Do you write AI-generated content?
We use AI for research and analysis. The deliverable isn't generic generated content — it's structured pages, sharper evidence, better answers, useful resources, schema, and authority signals buyers and models can both trust.
Why do third-party mentions matter so much?
AI infers trust from the wider web. Reviews, communities, publications, videos, partner profiles, and comparison pages all influence whether your brand gets recommended or ignored — often more than your own homepage does.
How do you measure whether this works?
Share-of-answer across the prompts that matter, source citations, organic rankings, referral sessions from AI platforms, engagement quality, assisted conversions, and the specific pages and prompts producing commercial opportunities.
